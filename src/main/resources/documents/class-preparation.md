# Preparing for Class

## What to wear

Wear comfortable clothing you can move and stretch in freely — for example
leggings or shorts and a fitted top. Avoid clothes that are too loose, as they
can get in the way during inversions or forward folds. Yoga is practiced
barefoot, so no special footwear is needed.

## What to bring

- A water bottle to stay hydrated.
- A small towel, especially for warmer or more active classes.
- Your own mat, if you prefer. 
- 
Props such as blocks, straps, and bolsters are available at the studio.

## When to arrive

Arrive at least 10 minutes before the class starts. This gives you
time to change, set up your space, and settle your breathing before practice
begins. Late entry may not be possible once a class has started.

## Eating before class

Avoid a heavy meal in the two to three hours before class, as practicing on a
full stomach is uncomfortable, especially during twists and forward bends. A
light snack beforehand is fine if you need energy.

## Preparing for a hot yoga class

Hot yoga is practiced in a heated room, so it needs a little extra preparation:

- Hydrate well throughout the day, not just right before class.
- Bring a towel and a full water bottle — you will sweat significantly more than
  in a regular class.
- Wear light, breathable clothing.
- Avoid eating for at least two hours before class.
- If you feel dizzy or unwell at any point, rest and drink water. It is
  completely fine to sit out part of the class.

If it is your first hot yoga class, let the instructor know so they can keep an
eye on you.

## Arriving with an injury or concern

Always tell your instructor before class about injuries, pain, pregnancy, or any
health condition. They will offer modifications so you can practice safely.
Never push into pain — discomfort is not a goal in yoga.

## Etiquette

- Silence your phone before entering the practice space.
- Keep the space calm and quiet out of respect for others.
- If you must leave early, let the instructor know beforehand and set up near the
  exit.
